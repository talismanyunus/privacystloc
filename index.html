PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 · ST LOC for iOS

This policy explains how ST LOC processes data for accounts, place discovery, and sharing with friends. For privacy questions and data requests, contact yusahmedia@gmail.com.

1. Data we process and why

DataPurpose
Name or nickname, username, profile icon, account ID, and friend codeTo create your account, display your profile, and help friends find you. New account setup does not require an email address or password; it creates a secure session on your device. If you sign in to an existing email-based account, your email address is processed for authentication.
Friend requests and connectionsTo manage requests and restrict shared information to accepted friends. Basic profile information may appear when finding users and sending friend requests.
Precise location, measurement time, and speedWith your permission, location is used for maps, nearby places, routes, and weather. Your latest location and speed are sent to the server when you separately enable friend sharing. Vehicle status is inferred from speed and may be inaccurate.
Friend labels and alert preferencesClose friend, partner, and family labels, plus foreground movement and proximity preferences, are stored on your device. Optional server alert preferences, the latest approximate pair distance and alert cooldown are stored on the server to detect movement and arrival. Labels do not grant access to another person’s location.
Optional friend notificationsIf you enable notifications, a device notification token and your selected app language are registered with your account so notifications can be delivered in that language. Per-friend nudge permissions default to off. The server processes the sender, recipient, event time and delivery status to send an authorized nudge or an opted-in movement/proximity alert. Notification text does not contain precise location or the sender’s name.
Last active timeIf you enable this option, your last active time is stored on the server and shown to accepted friends.
Place check-ins and ratingsWhen you share a check-in, its place name, category, rating, and time are displayed in the friends feed.
Favorites, personal reviews, collections, search and visit history, and offline areasThese are stored on your device in the current version. Place check-ins you intentionally share to the friends feed are separate.
Technical connection information and support correspondenceServers may process your IP address and request information when you connect, for security, service operation, and troubleshooting. Support emails are processed to respond to your request.

Optional links to Google Maps, Kentkart and Antalyakart open external services. Opening a place link sends the selected business name and address to Google Maps, not your friends’ locations. Those services apply their own privacy policies. ST LOC does not import their ratings or live bus arrival data.

2. Permissions and sharing controls

Granting location permission does not automatically share your location with friends. Location/speed sharing and last active sharing are separate settings, both off by default during setup. Optional background location sharing requires a separate in-app switch and Always location permission. It may update your shared location and speed while the screen is locked or the app is in the background, without changing your last active time. It can increase battery use. Updates depend on iOS, network access and fresh location measurements; force quitting the app or turning off the phone stops updates.

You can turn sharing off in the app’s Settings. Once the app connects to the server, the relevant latest location/speed or last active record is cleared. If you are offline, this may be delayed until a connection is available. You can also revoke location permission in iOS Settings. Removing a friend ends access through that connection; information already seen or captured in screenshots cannot be withdrawn.

You can withdraw a friend’s nudge permission in their details and disable notifications in iOS Settings. Removing a friendship clears its nudge permission. Movement and proximity animations work while the app is open. Separately enabled server alerts can notify a recipient whose app is closed when fresh shared measurements arrive. Movement requires a fresh location from the friend; proximity requires fresh locations from both people. Alerts may take about a minute and are not continuous background tracking; a family label is not a child safety monitoring service.

3. Service providers and data transfers

  • Supabase: Authentication and cloud database services. The current project is hosted in Tokyo, Japan; your data may be processed outside your country of residence. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Apple Push Notification service: For enabled friend notifications, device tokens and notification content are sent to Apple for delivery. Display, sound and vibration depend on device settings. Apple privacy policy.
  • Apple MapKit: Maps, place search, and routing. Searches and necessary location information may be processed by Apple services. Apple privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo: Coordinates are sent to this service for weather requests. Open-Meteo terms and privacy information.
  • Google/Gmail: Emails sent to our support address are received through Gmail. Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: When this page is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical visit information under its own policy. GitHub privacy statement.

The app does not use an advertising tracking SDK. This support page contains no analytics scripts, advertisements, or cookie-based plugins.

4. Retention and deletion

For optional nudges, the delivery worker removes notification device registrations after 30 days without renewal and notification event records after one day. Server alert preferences and detection state are cleared when a friendship or account is removed; withdrawing location sharing clears pending activity alerts and pair-distance state. Deleting your account removes these records from the active database. Relationship labels and foreground alert preferences stay on your device.

Profile, friendship, and check-in records are retained to provide the service while your account exists. Each new location measurement replaces the latest record; no separate route history is created for friend sharing. Successful account deletion removes your account, profile, friend requests and connections, location, last active time, and check-ins from the active database. It also clears the app’s local records and session on your device.

Service providers’ security logs and any backups may follow their own retention processes; we do not promise that all such records are erased simultaneously. Support correspondence is kept for as long as necessary to resolve the request and, where applicable, meet legal obligations. Contact us with questions about retention or the scope of deletion.

5. Your rights and security

You can contact our support address to request access, correction, deletion, or to object to processing under applicable law. Account ownership verification may be necessary to prevent changes to someone else’s account. We will not ask you to email your password or session token. We use HTTPS and account-based access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children and policy updates

The current version of ST LOC does not provide a dedicated child-tracking service. Contact support if you have a concern about a child’s personal data. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date and provide in-app notice when appropriate.

ACCOUNT AND DATA

Account deletion and privacy choices

  1. Open Settings → Account → My account in ST LOC.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Review the data to be deleted and confirm permanent deletion. An internet connection is required.

In the Turkish app interface, these labels are “Ayarlar → Hesap → Hesabım” and “Hesabımı sil”.

Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account from the server. Account deletion cannot be undone.

To stop sharing without deleting your account, turn off location and last active sharing in Settings. To clear only local records, use Settings → My records → Clear records (“Ayarlar → Kayıtlarım → Kayıtları temizle”).

If you cannot access the app, contact us for account deletion assistance. Include your username and a description of the issue, but do not send your precise location or password. If a device-based, email-free session has been lost, a username alone does not prove account ownership; additional verification may be necessary.

SUPPORT

How can we help?

Contact us with bug reports, privacy requests, or suggestions.

yusahmedia@gmail.com

For bug reports, include your iPhone model, iOS and app versions, and the steps that led to the issue. Hide private information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, session tokens, or precise location information.

Why can’t I see my friend on the map?

Your friend request must be accepted. Your friend must enable both location permission and in-app location sharing, and have an internet connection to send fresh measurements. Optional background sharing additionally requires the in-app switch and Always location permission; updates are not guaranteed after force quitting.

Can I sign in on a new phone using my username?

An email-free account relies on the secure session stored on your device. Username-only account recovery is not available in the current version. Keep this limitation in mind before losing or signing out of that session.

Are speed and traffic information exact?

No. GPS, connectivity, and map provider data can introduce delays or errors. Vehicle status is an estimate. ST LOC is not an emergency or safety monitoring system. Do not interact with your phone while driving.
